A letter for you

My Mohid,

Happy birthday to my Mohid, my favourite person, my safest place, and honestly one of the most beautiful things that has ever happened to me. I don't even know where to begin because there is so much I want to say to you and somehow every time I try to put it into words, words just feel too small. You have become such an important part of my life that sometimes I genuinely wonder how my days used to feel before you were in them. You are not just someone I love, you are someone I look for in everything. In the little things that happen during my day, in the things I want to tell someone first, in the random thoughts that come into my head, somehow it is always you. You have this way of making ordinary moments feel special just by being there. Even the simplest conversations with you mean more to me than you probably realise. I love your voice, your way of talking, your silly little habits, the way you make me laugh when I am not even in the mood to smile, and even the things you do that annoy me because somehow I would still choose all of it again. I love knowing that somewhere in this huge world there is a person who feels like home to me, and that person is you.

Sometimes I sit and think about how lucky I am that out of all the people in the world, somehow our paths crossed. There are billions of people, billions of stories, and somehow mine became connected to yours. I don't think you understand how much that means to me. You have seen different versions of me, the happy one, the emotional one, the annoying one, the dramatic one, the sleepy one, the overthinking one, and somehow you are still here. You have listened to me talk about the most random things and somehow made me feel like they mattered. You have given me comfort without even knowing how badly I needed it sometimes. You have made me feel loved in ways I did not even know I needed to be loved. And I hope you know that I notice it. I notice the little things. I notice when you remember something I said ages ago. I notice when you try to make me feel better. I notice when you stay even when I am being difficult. I notice every little effort, even the ones you probably think are too small to matter. They matter to me. You matter to me. More than I can explain properly.

You know how much you love poetry, so maybe if I were a poet, I would write pages and pages about you. I would write about how somehow your name sounds softer when it is mine to say. I would write about how some people enter your life like a passing season, but some people become the reason you start looking forward to every season, and you became that person for me. If I could turn my feelings into poetry, I think every verse would somehow end with your name. Maybe I would write, "Mohid, tum woh shakhs ho jise lafzon mein bayan karna mushkil hai, kyun ke jo dil mein basa ho, usay alfaaz mein kaise sameta ja sakta hai." And maybe I would write about your smile, your eyes, your presence, and all the little things about you that I have fallen for without even realising when it happened. You are the kind of person who makes me believe that some people really are written into our lives for a reason. And if there is such a thing as being written together somewhere, then I hope my name is somewhere close to yours.

On your birthday, more than anything, I want you to know how proud I am of you. I hope this year brings you closer to every dream you have, even the ones you have not told me about yet. I hope you get the kind of happiness that stays even after the excitement of a good day is gone. I hope you find peace in the moments when life gets difficult. I hope you never forget how capable you are, even on the days when you doubt yourself. I want to see you grow, succeed, become everything you have ever wanted to become, and I want to be there clapping the loudest when you do. I want to see you achieve things that once seemed impossible to you. I want to hear you tell me about your achievements with that happiness in your voice. And when life does not go according to plan, I want you to remember that you will never have to face everything alone. I will always want to be the person you can come back to, the person you can talk to, the person you can be completely yourself around without worrying about being judged.

I wish I could give you everything you deserve today. I wish I could put every beautiful thing in the world into one little box and give it to you, because even that would still feel like it is not enough. So instead, I am giving you my words, my love, my prayers, and all the feelings I sometimes struggle to say out loud. I pray that Allah protects you, keeps you happy, gives you a long and beautiful life, opens doors for you that you never imagined would open, and keeps your heart soft even when life gives you reasons to become hard. I pray that every year of your life becomes better than the one before it. I pray that you are surrounded by people who genuinely love you and want the best for you. And selfishly, I pray that I get to remain one of those people for a very, very long time.

I hope one day when we look back at this birthday, we laugh at how young we were and how much we thought we knew about life. I hope we have countless birthdays to celebrate together, countless cakes, countless late night conversations, countless stupid arguments that we eventually laugh about, countless pictures that we will be embarrassed to look at later, and countless memories that only we will understand. I want to know all the versions of you that are still waiting to exist. I want to know the person you become at twenty, twenty five, thirty and every age after that. I want to see how your dreams change, how your laugh changes, how life changes you, and I hope through all of those changes, I am still somewhere beside you. Maybe older, maybe more mature, maybe still annoying you for absolutely no reason, but still there.

Mohid, if I had to choose one thing to wish for you today, it would be that you never forget how loved you are. Not just by me, but by the people whose lives you have touched in ways you may never realise. You deserve to know that your existence matters. Your presence matters. Your dreams matter. Your happiness matters. And to me, you matter in a way that is difficult to explain without sounding like I am exaggerating, but I am not. I love you in the quiet moments, in the loud moments, when we are laughing, when we are arguing, when we are talking for hours, and even when we have nothing to say. I love you in all the little ordinary moments that probably mean nothing to anyone else but somehow mean everything to me. You have become a part of my heart so naturally that I cannot remember when it happened. I just know that one day you were there, and then suddenly you were everywhere.

So happy birthday, my love. Happy birthday to the person who somehow became my favourite notification, my favourite conversation, my favourite person to bother, my favourite person to miss, and my favourite person to love. I hope today reminds you of just how special you are. I hope you smile a lot. I hope you eat something you love. I hope you feel celebrated. And I hope somewhere between all the birthday wishes you receive, you remember that there is one girl sitting somewhere who is ridiculously grateful that you were born. Because if you had not been born, I would have never gotten to know you, never gotten to love you, never gotten to call you mine, and honestly, I cannot imagine my life without the version of it that has you in it.

Happy birthday, Mohid. May your life be as beautiful as the poetry you love, and may I always have a place somewhere in the story. I love you more than these words can hold, and if I had another thousand pages, I would still find something new to say about you. Happy birthday, my baby. ❤️

Always your baby,
me ❤️

for whenever you need me

Open when...

Open when you're sad 🥺
I know I cannot magically fix everything that is making you sad, but I want you to remember that you don't have to pretend to be okay around me. You can tell me everything, even the things you think are stupid or too small. I will listen. I will stay. And if all you need is someone to sit quietly with you, then I can be that person too. Please remember that bad days do not define your life. This feeling will pass. Until it does, you have me. Come here, birthday boy. You are loved more than you know. ❤️
Open when you're happy 🥰
I hope you are smiling while reading this because knowing you are happy is one of my favourite things. Whatever happened, I hope you enjoy every second of it. Tell me about it. Send me ten messages about it if you want. I want to hear your excitement. Your happiness makes me happy too, and I hope life gives you thousands of reasons to smile like this.
Open when you miss me 🫶🏻
If you miss me right now, then congratulations because I probably miss you too. Just imagine me sitting there beside you, annoying you, stealing your attention and refusing to let you be serious for five minutes. Until I can actually be there, consider this little note my hug. You are stuck with me, unfortunately. There is no return policy. ❤️
Open when you're angry at me 😭
Okay first of all, breathe. Second of all, I probably deserve at least some of your anger because I am not always the easiest person to deal with. But please do not let one bad moment make you forget everything good between us. Tell me what is wrong instead of keeping it inside. We can argue, we can disagree, we can get annoyed with each other, but I never want you to doubt how much I love you. Now come back to me when you're done being mad. I have decided that you are allowed to be angry for exactly five business minutes.
Open when you need motivation 🌙
You are capable of so much more than you sometimes give yourself credit for. I hope you keep going even when things feel slow. You do not have to have everything figured out today. Take your time, keep moving, and remember why you started. I believe in you. And when you finally get where you wanted to go, I hope you look back and realise that every difficult day was worth surviving.
Open when you can't sleep 🌙
Since I cannot magically appear beside you and tell you to sleep, this note will have to do. Put your phone down after reading this, close your eyes and stop thinking about absolutely everything at once. And if your brain refuses to cooperate, just imagine us somewhere peaceful, talking about completely random things until one of us falls asleep. Goodnight, my love. Sleep properly. You have a birthday boy reputation to maintain tomorrow.
Open when you want to know how much I love you ❤️
More than I know how to explain. More than I manage to say. More than you probably realise when we are just talking about completely random things. I love you in the ordinary moments. I love you when you make me laugh. I love you when you annoy me. I love you when we talk for hours and I love you even when we have absolutely nothing to say. I love the person you are now, and I want to know every version of the person you are going to become. So if you ever wonder whether you are loved, come back here and read this again. You are. Completely. ❤️
one last thing

I love you,
Mohid.

Out of all the people I could have met, somehow I met you. Out of all the stories I could have lived, somehow mine became connected to yours. And if I could choose again, I would still choose you.
